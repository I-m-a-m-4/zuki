faq

frequently asked questions

everything you need to know about zuki on windows, privacy, and models.

what is zuki?

zuki is an open-source, voice-first desktop companion for Windows 10 & 11. Hold your hotkey (Ctrl+Shift+Space), speak naturally, and zuki sees what you see on your monitor. It walks you through complex apps, draws guidance overlays right on your screen, or autonomously clicks and types to execute multi-step workflows.

how is zuki different from macOS heyclicky?

heyclicky is exclusively for macOS Sonoma. zuki is purpose-built from the ground up for Windows 10 & 11. It utilizes native Windows UI Automation (UIA) accessibility trees to snap to exact element coordinates, integrates with COM desktop shell APIs, and runs lightweight in your Windows system tray.

is zuki recording my screen all the time?

absolutely not. zuki never captures your screen unless you deliberately press your push-to-talk hotkey. When you release, audio and vision capture immediately cease. No background video feeds or spyware.

can i bring my own API keys?

yes! zuki is 100% open-source under the MIT license. You can plug in your own Anthropic Claude 3.5 Sonnet, Groq, OpenAI, or OpenRouter keys in the settings dialog and use zuki completely free forever with no limits.

how does computer use work without breaking things?

zuki uses a dual-engine architecture: Fast Regex + Windows UI Automation for instant zero-latency safe actions, paired with Claude 3.5 Sonnet's Computer Use for complex visual reasoning. Destructive actions (like deleting files or sending emails) always ask for your spoken or hotkey confirmation first.

what are the system requirements?

Any PC running Windows 10 (version 1903+) or Windows 11 with 4GB+ RAM and an active internet connection. No expensive GPU required, as model reasoning runs over high-speed APIs.
